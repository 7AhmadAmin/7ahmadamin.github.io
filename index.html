About / manifesto · № 02关于 / 宣言 · № 02

I design forensic AI that is useful, explainable, and deployable.我致力于设计有用、可解释、可部署的取证人工智能。

My research addresses real-world cybersecurity and AI-safety challenges at the intersection of trustworthy machine learning, computer vision, and multimedia security. I develop robust, interpretable, and fair AI systems for deepfake detection, forensics, biometric anti-spoofing, and privacy-preserving AI.我的研究关注可信机器学习、计算机视觉与多媒体安全交叉处的真实网络安全与 AI 安全问题，目标是构建鲁棒、可解释、公平的深度伪造检测、多媒体取证、生物特征防伪与隐私保护 AI 系统。

I am interested in human-centered, higher-level, human-friendly ML concepts that make model behavior understandable to researchers, investigators, engineers, and the wider public.我关注以人为中心、更高层次、易于理解的机器学习表达方式，使研究人员、调查人员、工程师和公众都能理解模型行为。

Research areas · № 03研究方向 · № 03

01

AI-driven cybersecurity & forensicsAI驱动的网络安全与取证

02

Trustworthy & privacy-preserving AI可信与隐私保护AI

Fairness, interpretability, adversarial robustness, secure data pipelines, and transparent forensic reasoning.公平性、可解释性、对抗鲁棒性、安全数据管道与透明取证推理。

03

Learning under data distribution shift数据分布偏移下的学习

Unsupervised, semi-supervised, and self-supervised learning for threat environments that differ from curated datasets.面向与精心整理的数据集不同的威胁环境的无监督、半监督与自监督学习。

04

Multimodal and higher-level evidence多模态与高层级证据

Alternative color spaces, frequency statistics, temporal coherence, audio-visual cues, VLMs, and LLM-driven workflows.替代色彩空间、频域统计、时序一致性、音视频线索、视觉语言模型与大语言模型驱动的工作流。

Current Research Questions当前研究问题

Can deepfake predictions be explained in an interpretable way? Do similar detectors use different evidence? Can black-box explanations match model-specific explanations? Can temporal and audio information yield more coherent explanations? How can explanations improve models and datasets?深度伪造检测的预测能否以可解释方式呈现？相似检测器是否使用不同证据？黑盒解释能否接近模型特定解释？时序与音频信息能否产生更一致的解释？解释性如何反向改进模型与数据集？
